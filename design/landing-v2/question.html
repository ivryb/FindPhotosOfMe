Questions organizers ask.

Do attendees need to install anything?

No. They open your event's link in a browser, sign in, and add a selfie. If your community uses Telegram, they can also send the selfie to a bot.

Do attendees pay?

Never. You pay once for the event, and searching and downloading is free for everyone you share the link with.

Is there a subscription?

No. It's one payment per event. If you run another event next year, you pay for that one then.

Does it find people in group photos?

Yes. Every face in every photo is indexed, so a face in the crowd or the back row is searchable. Very small, blurred, or covered faces are harder to match.

Will it find every photo of someone?

Most, not always all. Clear faces in decent light match well. Profiles, motion blur, masks, and sunglasses can cause misses, and attendees can try a second selfie if the first one finds too little.

How long does it take to set up?

Creating the event takes a couple of minutes. Processing depends on how many photos you upload, and you can watch its progress. Share the link once it shows as ready.

Who can see the photos?

Attendees sign in before searching, and each person's results are visible only to them. Your event page shows the event's name and description, not the whole gallery.

I'm an attendee, not the organizer. Can I set this up?

Yes. If you have the folder, you can create the event yourself, upload the photos, and share the link with everyone who was there. That's how this started.
