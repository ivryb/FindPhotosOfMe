Why I built this

After a conference, the organizers shared a Google Drive folder with about 5,000 photos. I wanted to find the ones I was in. Scrolling through all of them was miserable.

So I wrote a small tool that found every face in the folder and searched it with my selfie. It worked. The next year I made it into a website and a Telegram bot for everyone at the conference, and people loved it.

Now anyone can use it for their own event.

Ivan RybnikovCreator of FindPhotosOfMe

Questions

What kinds of events does it work for?

Any event with a photographer: conferences, meetups, offsites, weddings, parties. Races are harder because of motion, caps, and sunglasses, so try it free on your race photos first.

Do people need to install anything?

No. They open your event's link in a browser, or send a selfie to the event's bot in Telegram or WhatsApp.

Do people pay to search?

No. You pay once for the event. Searching is free for everyone.

Is there a subscription?

No. It's one payment per event. If you run another event next year, you pay for that one then.

Does it find people in group photos?

Yes. We find every face in every photo, including people in the crowd or the back row. Very small, blurry, or covered faces are harder to match.

Will it find every photo of someone?

Most of them, but not always all. Clear faces in good light work best. Side views, blur, masks, and sunglasses can cause misses. People can try another selfie if the first one finds too few.

How long does it take to set up?

Creating the event takes a couple of minutes. Processing the photos takes longer the more photos you have, and you can watch the progress. Share the link once it's done.

Who can see the photos?

Anyone with your event's link can look through the photos. People sign in before searching with a selfie, and only they see what they found.

I wasn't the organizer. Can I set this up?

Yes. If you have the photos, you can create the event, upload them, and share the link with everyone who was there. That's how this started.
