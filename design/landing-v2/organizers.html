Questions

For organizers

What do I upload, and how many photos can I add?

ZIP files with JPEG or PNG photos. Each ZIP can be up to 2 GB with up to 10,000 files, and you can add more ZIPs later. Each plan has a photo limit and a total size: 100 photos and 2 GB on the free trial, 1,000 and 5 GB on Small event, 5,000 and 25 GB on Event, and 20,000 and 100 GB on Large event.

What counts as a search?

Each selfie someone checks against your photos is one search. Every plan includes a number of searches, and the people searching never pay. If you run out, you can add 1,000 more searches for $15.

How long does processing take?

It depends on how many photos you upload. Each ZIP shows its progress, and searching works as soon as it says Ready.

How long does my event stay online?

7 days on the free trial, 30 days on Small event, and 90 days on Event and Large event. After that, searching stops.

Who can see the photos?

Anyone with the event link can open its page, see the preview photos you chose, and search with a selfie. Search matches the face in the submitted photo; it doesn’t check who that person is.

How do I set up the Telegram bot?

Create a bot with @BotFather in Telegram, copy its token, and paste it into your event settings. Then share the bot link along with your event link.

For attendees

What kind of selfie should I use?

A clear, well-lit photo of one face, looking at the camera. If there are several faces in the photo, the search uses only one of them. JPEG, PNG, or WebP, up to 10 MB.

Does it find people in group photos?

Yes, search can find you in group shots too. Small or blurry faces, side views, and sunglasses can make matching harder.

Can it miss photos or show someone else?

Yes. Search can miss a photo or include someone who looks similar. A clear, well-lit selfie helps. Try another photo if you’re not finding the shots you expected.

Do I need an account to search?

No. Open the event link and add a selfie. If the event has a photo bot, you can send your selfie there instead.

I’m not the organizer. Can I still upload the photos?

Yes, if you have permission to upload and share them. Create an event and share the link with the other attendees.
