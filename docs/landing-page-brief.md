# Landing-page brief

Use this brief when changing the landing page or its public claims. Check product behavior in the app before turning prototype copy into a promise.

## The offer

FindPhotosOfMe helps attendees find photos of themselves in an event collection with a selfie. Organizers or photographers upload the collection and share the event link. Lead with conferences and community events.

Explain the product with an event gallery, a reference photo, and matching results. Keep the organizer's role clear even when the headline addresses attendees. Use [Product and pricing](portfolio-launch.md#product-and-pricing) for the commercial offer.

A working headline is "Selfie search for event photos." Supporting copy can explain the action: "Upload your event photos and share one link. Attendees search with a selfie and download their matches."

## Design references

The [current variations](../design/landing-v2/index.html) include the [combined concept](../design/landing-v2/combined.html). Use them when discussing the next iteration. They are local prototypes, not an approved production specification.

The [earlier concepts](../design/landing/index.html) and their adjacent rationale files preserve the Gather, Spotlight, and Keepsake explorations. There is no need to generate those concepts again or follow their old section counts and palettes.

Show the search experience early. Explain how an organizer uploads, waits for processing, and shares the link. Then explain how attendees search and download. Use event photographs to illustrate the audiences, followed by pricing and practical questions. Choose sections that answer a buyer's questions rather than meeting a section quota.

## Product claims

- Browser search requires sign-in. Include that step when explaining the attendee flow.
- Telegram search works through an event's configured bot. Keep bot availability specific to the event.
- Matches can be wrong or incomplete. Publish timing and capacity claims only after testing representative collections.
- Public event pages show selected preview images. They do not currently offer a browser for the full collection.
- WhatsApp is not implemented. The switch and copy in the combined prototype describe a proposed feature.
- The prototype's paid prices remain drafts. Confirm them against the billing decision and live checkout before publishing purchase buttons.

Access controls restrict stored search results to the requesting account. They do not prove that the submitted face belongs to that person. Describe the actual access, storage, and deletion behavior instead of promising that only the photographed person can find a photo.

Use Ivan's account for the founder story. Confirm additions about dates, adoption, or audience response with him. Event logos, testimonials, and performance figures need their own evidence or permission.

## Demos and release checks

Label simulated searches and sample counts. Use the same person in the reference photo and sample results. Record image sources and permission to use them.

Prototype controls should perform a local action or explain that the feature is a preview. Production buttons must reach working sign-in, event creation, or checkout flows. Publish confirmed contact details and policies.

Before publishing, check the page on mobile and desktop, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, image loading, and every link. Resolve the WhatsApp, full-gallery, and pricing differences above. Test the complete organizer and attendee flows before describing them as ready for new customers.
